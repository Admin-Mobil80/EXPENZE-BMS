Platform settings

Channels

One number and one mailbox serve every customer. The sender is resolved to an organisation, so these are the same for everyone.

Trial

Granted once, when an organisation is created. Enough to audit a real week of receipts and form a view — not enough to run on.

Low-credit alerts

Whichever is crossed first. Two thresholds because organisations run out differently: a company filing four receipts a month wants warning at a low number, one filing four hundred wants a week's notice whatever the number. Sent by email, and on WhatsApp to anyone who has verified a number. Once per crossing — it re-arms when the balance recovers.

Indian tax

Added on top of the INR price at checkout and shown to the customer as its own line, never folded into the price. USD sales are an export of services and are not taxed here.
